oscar-wallpaper privacy policy

oscar-wallpaper is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to other people.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Photos Library API with access limited to what the app itself creates. It creates one album per TV, uploads the wallpaper images it renders into those albums, and removes earlier wallpapers from them. It cannot see or change any other photos or albums in the account.

What it stores

The Google sign-in token, the IDs of the albums it created, and the images it rendered are kept on the owner's home server. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google Photos and to the TVs on the home network.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page (myaccount.google.com/connections).
